How I work

What I bring to a search.

Closing hard-to-fill, senior, and specialist roles by partnering directly with CXOs and business heads to define what "right" actually looks like — before sourcing starts.

Manpower forecasting and talent mapping done ahead of need, not after a role opens — so critical positions never start from zero.

Generative AI for JD drafting, sourcing, and shortlisting, turned into offline-usable templates and dashboards — the speed of automation without losing hiring judgment.

EVP, referral programs, and onboarding design that shows up in the numbers that matter — lower early attrition, not just faster offers.
